# Stage Gates

Advance when the information needed for the next decision is reliable enough.
Do not demand complete documentation before useful work can begin.

The bundled checker validates durable workspace files. Specialist skill input,
current PostPlus CLI schemas, provider validation, quotes, permissions, and live
state remain separate execution requirements.

## Onboarding

Require a resolved folder, brand and product identity, product category and
description, default market and platform, and indexed source materials.

Product truth remains pending until a human confirms it.

## Research

Require product identity and category, a research question, market or platform
scope, and separation between first-party facts and external findings.

Approved claims are not required merely to collect market evidence.

## Strategy

Require sufficient product truth for the proposed angle, one target segment,
trigger event, campaign objective, KPI, and claim and proof boundaries.

Block any strategy that depends on an unsupported claim.

## Test

Require traceable hypothesis IDs, coherent test cells, changed and controlled
variables, a primary KPI, evidence threshold, winner, loser, and inconclusive
rules.

Human approval of the test plan is required before production.

## Production

Require approved product truth, approved test plan, resolved identity-critical
references, a brief linked to a hypothesis, duration, aspect ratio, resolution,
audio configuration, and current provider or workflow validation.

## Launch

Require clean validation, final creative review, current quote, cost ceiling,
destination and account identity, and explicit launch or publishing approval.

Creative approval is not launch approval.

## Learning

Require the test plan, creative IDs, normalized performance data, enough
evidence for the stated conclusion, and a clear distinction between observation,
interpretation, and next hypothesis.

Mark insufficient or confounded results as inconclusive.

## Mandatory Human Approvals

Keep mandatory approval to three decisions:

1. Product truth
2. Test plan
3. Spend, launch, publishing, or live changes

Additional review may be useful, but do not create unnecessary gates.
