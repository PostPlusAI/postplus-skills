# Uncertainty Policy

## Decision Test

Ask whether missing or conflicting information could change:

1. Product truth or advertising claims
2. Audience, strategy, or test meaning
3. Person, product, or asset identity
4. Workflow structure or provider behavior
5. Compliance, permission, spend, publishing, or live state

If yes, stop the affected step and ask the smallest necessary question.

## Hard Blockers

Stop for:

- unresolved product or SKU identity
- unsupported efficacy, medical, legal, financial, guarantee, or comparative claims
- conflicting official sources
- missing exact product reference when visible appearance must be preserved
- missing or conflicting narrator identity when continuity matters
- unclear asset permission for external use
- missing objective, offer, or CTA when it changes the output
- missing hypothesis or test meaning before batch production
- missing approval before semantic truth changes, spend, launch, publishing, or live mutation

Continue unrelated low-risk work while waiting.

## Safe Defaults

Choose and record a default only when it is reversible, low risk, independent of
product truth and test meaning, and covered by a current platform convention.

Examples include ordinary props, neutral wardrobe, minor camera movement,
practical lighting, file naming, and internal formatting.

## Repair Boundary

Repair automatically:

- formatting
- missing mechanical identifiers
- broken local references with one unambiguous target
- duration arithmetic
- naming mismatches with one authoritative source
- clearly duplicated text

Propose a patch and ask before repairing:

- product facts, claims, price, or offer
- audience priority or persona identity
- asset duties or permissions
- hypothesis meaning, KPI, or success rules
- approval state
- spend, launch, publishing, or live configuration

## Question Format

```text
BLOCKER: <missing or conflicting fact>
IMPACT: <what decision or output it changes>
NEEDED: <the smallest concrete confirmation>
CONTINUING: <unblocked work that can continue>
```

Do not ask broad questions such as "Please provide more information."
