# Video report

For an open-ended analysis, omit `--prompt`. The service supplies the default:
a shot table covering time, visual, framing/composition, camera motion, action,
offer/prop relationship, lighting/color, sound, speech/text, and purpose;
followed by Continuity Locks, Proof Status, Claim Boundaries, and Causal Closure Gate.
Shots should support still generation, video prompting, or animation production,
with one dominant action and one causal idea each.

For a specific question, pass that question in `--prompt`. It replaces the default;
do not force a shot table, unrelated sections, minimum length, or full-duration
coverage onto a narrow request. Images and native carousels have no video template.

Read and deliver the original Markdown. Report observed limitations and keep
Agent interpretation separate. A completed response is not proof that every
statement is accurate; do not purchase a second analysis to repair its wording.
