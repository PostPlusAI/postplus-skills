# Plan the next creative slate and learn from its results

Use existing account findings, customer language and validated external
benchmarks. Keep each source's identity, dates, buyer context and limitations.
An ad running for a long time or a popular organic post is a research signal,
not proof of profitable paid acquisition. Approved product facts own claims;
customer comments do not become permitted testimonials automatically.

## Decide what to make

Define a concept by audience situation, motivation, angle and format, with its
source attached. Rank evidence and business relevance before production effort.
Own-account qualified results usually support a closer iteration than an
unverified competitor pattern; consider comparability rather than assign
universal numeric weights or format tiers.

When no effective concept is established, explore distinct needs/angles with
bounded production effort. When a concept has mature useful outcomes, extend
it with meaningfully different executions while retaining room for new ideas.
Do not require historical winners for a new product or polish only losing
directions. Match production fidelity to evidence, available assets, rights,
capacity and the user's budget. Use actual current generation quotes when
production is requested; do not embed a provider price list here.

Deliver a feasible slate: concept ID, source, buyer situation, hypothesis,
format, proof, changed/held variables, production effort, success metric and
owner/next action. The slate fits available capacity; do not force a fixed ad
count, format mix or explore/scale percentage.

## Connect the execution to the learning

Preserve concept, hypothesis, creative and ad identities through existing
project/test records. Ads owns delivery data, attribution scope and paid
experiments; this skill uses those findings to revise the production slate.
Do not claim that a particular hook caused a lift from an unevenly delivered
ad comparison.

Inspect where the observed path weakened: opening attention, continued viewing,
click, landing action or qualified customer. Treat each diagnosis as a
hypothesis until evidence distinguishes the component from audience, placement,
event quality or offer. Keep metric definitions and denominators visible.
Do not label a high-attention, low-quality ad a winner or treat several failed
executions as conclusive proof that an entire buyer need is invalid.

Revise the brief, actual hook/body transition or required proof when within
scope; hand a complete production object to the appropriate existing media
skill. Preserve user references, dialogue and approved creative boundaries.
Generated video contains picture and needed dialogue; text overlays, captions,
stickers and end cards remain separate postproduction unless explicitly asked.

## Close the cycle

Record observations, alternative explanations, retired/retained directions and
the next test in the existing results and strategy files. Update the next slate
from those findings; do not create a second product-truth or approval store.
Retain the old brief and asset IDs for comparison. A report does not authorize
generation, campaign mutation, sending or a recurring schedule.
