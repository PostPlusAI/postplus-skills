# TikTok Paid Examples

Use for public TikTok paid creative examples. Apply
[shared contract](shared-contract.md).

The current `tiktok-ads-top` route accepts `--limit` only. It cannot target a
brand, category, region, language, objective, or keyword. If the user requires
that targeting, explain the exact missing filter; do not pretend a general
sample satisfies it. Ask whether a general paid sample is useful only if that
change is needed to proceed. Never substitute organic content as paid proof.

```bash
postplus research run tiktok-ads-top --limit 30 --wait --output paid-examples.json
```

For an accepted general sample, keep actual returned fields and observation
dates. Visual/audio claims need selected-media analysis. Paid and organic
comparison runs separate evidence lanes; a repeated pattern is synthesis, not
proof of spend, targeting, objective, delivery performance, or ROAS. Missing
fields stay unknown.

Deliver useful examples, evidence-linked observations, scope limits, and cost.
Do not inflate a narrow paid sample to the broad organic research count. Use
standard report only for its supported records; preserve unsupported records as
raw evidence with a clear gap rather than pretending they rendered. End with
one concrete next action and its corresponding PostPlus skill.
