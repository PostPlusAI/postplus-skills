# X Campaign Scout

Use for launches, hashtags, slogans, mentions, brand activity, competitor
campaigns, public reactions, or a bounded campaign watchlist.

Apply `shared-contract.md` first.

## Run

1. Define `1-3` campaign seeds: hashtag, slogan, account, mention, product name,
   launch phrase, or competitor campaign.
2. Run one separately labeled `x-posts` query per seed with `20` posts.
3. Verify account identity with `x-profiles` only when authorship matters.
4. Use `audience-voice.md` only for selected reply threads.

Keep owned brand posts, third-party posts, and replies as separate evidence sets.

## Evidence

- A sample can show recurring messaging, participation, creative examples, and
  visible reaction patterns; it cannot prove full reach, lift, conversion, or spend.
- Mention volume in a bounded sample is not total campaign volume.
- Distinguish official accounts from recalled or similarly named accounts.

## Output

Return campaign scope, seeds, evidence sets, recurring messages and formats,
notable participants, public reaction themes when collected, gaps, and next action.
