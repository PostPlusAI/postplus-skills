# Writing and repurposing

Use the user's original article, interview, transcript, customer question, or
approved claims as the source of truth. A single source can support several
posts, but each post should stand on its own.

First extract the substantive point: audience problem, answer, proof, caveat,
and desired next step. Separate direct evidence from the author's opinion.
Mark numbers, quotes, client identities, and product results requiring source
or permission. When a useful point lacks proof, either express it as a
question/hypothesis or ask for the missing evidence.

Pick a structure that fits the point rather than filling a template:

- A lesson from an event: situation → choice → observed consequence → what the
  reader can apply.
- A practical explanation: problem → diagnosis → steps → boundary or example.
- An evidence-led claim: observation → source/method → interpretation →
  exception → useful action.
- A discussion prompt: concrete tension → the user's honest position → a
  question someone can actually answer.

Make the opening tell the reader what problem or outcome the post addresses.
Deliver that promise in the body. One clear call to action is enough. Keep
paragraphs, text overlay, links, tags, and hashtags purposeful. Do not assume
that moving a link to comments or posting at a purported “best hour” improves
reach; test those decisions against the user's own results.

For multi-platform adaptation, write a common claim ledger first, then adjust
the illustration and format: LinkedIn may need the business decision and its
trade-off; an Instagram image needs a clear visual and caption; Reddit needs a
direct answer in the community's context; Pinterest needs a searchable
topic/visual path; a video needs an observable scene. Avoid making identical
copy look “native” merely by changing hashtags.

Return reviewable drafts labeled by platform, proposed account, format,
opening, body, evidence/asset needed, link or next action, and unresolved
claim. If the user asks to publish, hand off to [channel execution](channel-execution.md)
and the appropriate platform reference. No account connection is necessary
to make a draft.
