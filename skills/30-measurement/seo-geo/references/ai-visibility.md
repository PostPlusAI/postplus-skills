# Measure AI answers and repair their source facts

Choose prompts that can change a business decision: discovery, suitability,
comparison, price, compatibility and purchase risk. Prefer real customer or
sales questions. Search terms can inform prompts but cannot be mechanically
expanded into a representative demand panel.

Keep a stable core panel and separate exploratory prompts. Record exact prompt,
engine/surface, search mode or model when observable, locale, market, date,
full answer and cited URLs. Record repeated observations and denominators for
small samples; one answer is an observation, not a stable visibility rate.
Version changes to the panel and compare common prompts when composition changes.
Do not blend different engines or locales into an unexplained average.

## Separate the outcomes

| Outcome | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Cited | A visible source link identifies the page in the captured answer |
| Mentioned | The answer names the brand/product |
| Recommended | The answer presents it as a suitable choice for the stated task; record conditions and negative recommendations |
| Accurate | Current authoritative facts support the actual feature, price, eligibility and limitation claims |

A citation can describe competitors rather than recommend the publisher. No
visible citation does not prove the engine never retrieved a page. Save answer
text and links instead of inferring hidden retrieval or training behavior.

## Correct a factual error

1. Capture the exact claim and context; distinguish false, stale, incomplete,
   ambiguous, subjective and unverifiable statements.
2. Check the approved product source and the cited page. Search for the likely
   stale source only when that distinction changes the repair; label unproven
   source attribution as a hypothesis.
3. Repair owned content through its actual authorized editor: update the
   owning pricing, docs or product fact and related visible/structured content.
   Preserve prior values and inspect the resulting page. Do not change the
   product or its price to match the answer.
4. Prepare a sourced correction for a third-party publisher where needed;
   sending it requires the user's messaging authorization. Do not fabricate
   reviews or public consensus.
5. Retest the same prompt cohort after an appropriate observation interval.
   Report changed answers and their sample size; do not promise that an owned
   page edit controls a model's next response.

## Use actual available evidence

Google's [AI site guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)
uses foundational SEO and does not require special AI files or schema. Search
Console now has [generative AI performance reports](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16984139);
do not repeat the obsolete claim that no dedicated report exists. The current
PostPlus GSC query tool has no dedicated AI-report operation or AI search-type
enum. Analyze a supplied official export, preserving its metric and missing-value
semantics, rather than inventing ai_mode/ai_overview inputs. Google AI display
data does not cover other assistants' citations or recommendations.

Use an authorized available browser/monitor or supplied answer captures for
other engines. If no sampling surface is available, deliver the prompt panel
and analyze existing captures, identifying what was not sampled. Do not replace
the requested engine's answer with a different model's simulated response.
Visible AI referral traffic is one signal; it does not capture all later brand
searches or prove incremental revenue. Use Measurement and business evidence
for the downstream question without summing platform credit.
