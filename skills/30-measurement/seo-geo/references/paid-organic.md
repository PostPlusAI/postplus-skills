# Combine paid and organic evidence into a decision

Use when deciding which demand to buy, which pages to strengthen or whether
paid and organic presence are overlapping. Ads owns campaign changes and paid
economics; Measurement owns GSC/GA4 reads and source definitions. Combine the
evidence already obtained before requesting another report.

Align site/account, country, language, device, dates, business event and lag.
Keep original search terms, matched bid keywords and GSC queries in their own
columns. Group by supported intent, not a guessed exact cross-platform join.
GSC query disclosure, Ads query disclosure and date conventions can differ.
Do not attach aggregate query conversions to an identifiable customer.

| Observation | Useful next step | What to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paid terms bring qualified outcomes but organic coverage is weak | Improve an existing relevant page or plan distinctive content | Mature customer outcomes, actual organic results, page capability and content effort |
| Organic queries show relevant demand without a useful paid test | Ask Ads to design a bounded experiment when paid acquisition is the goal | Query intent, allowable loss, event quality and real landing path |
| Both paid and organic appear strong for brand queries | Evaluate incremental value before changing spend | Competitor presence, customer outcomes and a suitable experiment; high organic position alone is not a pause rule |
| Clicks are high but qualified outcomes are weak | Locate the mismatch in event quality, traffic, offer or page | Tracking evidence through Measurement and exact paid configuration through Ads |

Paid performance is a selection signal, not proof that an organic page will
rank. Ads competition is not an organic difficulty score. Advertising does not
buy organic ranking preference. Google's
[paid and organic report](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3097241?hl=en)
requires linked accounts and has a particular text-ad scope; separate GAQL and
GSC extracts are not that native combined report or a randomized experiment.

Deliver one prioritized action queue: buyer need, paid evidence, organic page
evidence, qualified outcome evidence, content or ad action, effort and validation.
Transfer an approved campaign action to Ads using the same target and scope.
Use [content planning](content-planning.md) for page work and
[AI visibility](ai-visibility.md) to turn relevant buyer questions into an
observed AI-answer panel. Keep source data and the business decision distinct.
